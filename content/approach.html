A PRACTICAL PROCESS

What happens at each stage?

The work is agreed around your circumstances. Not every matter needs recruitment, and not every visa application involves an employer.

01

Initial discussion

We understand your needs and the basic facts, then identify whether a further assessment is appropriate.

02

Assessment and scope

We explain the issues to be reviewed, the service proposed and the work to be agreed before proceeding.

03

Preparation plan

A case-specific information list helps organise records, supporting documents and responsibilities across the people involved.

04

Case coordination

We review the preparation, follow up on gaps and coordinate communication within the agreed engagement.

05

Ongoing communication

We discuss relevant updates and information requests through the work, rather than leaving clients to connect the steps themselves.
