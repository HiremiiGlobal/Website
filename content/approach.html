A PRACTICAL PROCESS

What happens at each stage?

Each stage has a purpose and a part for you to play. Your preparation plan reflects the service agreed with the team.

01

Initial discussion

Tell us your goal, current circumstances and any deadlines. We clarify the enquiry and explain what information is needed for a closer assessment.

For youShare the background and your priorities.
02

Assessment and scope

Review the available background, discuss suitable service options and agree the scope, responsibilities and fees before proceeding.

For youReview and confirm the proposed engagement.
03

Preparation plan

Prepare an information checklist for your matter. We explain which records are needed and, where relevant, which items come from the employer or other parties.

For youGather the records listed for your matter.
04

Case coordination

Review the documents for gaps and consistency, clarify outstanding questions and coordinate the preparation with the people involved.

For youCheck factual details and provide missing information.
05

Ongoing communication

Explain relevant progress and requests for further information. If your circumstances change, discuss how that may affect the next steps within the agreed service.

For youTell us about changes to your circumstances.
