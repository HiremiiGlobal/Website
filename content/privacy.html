Privacy Policy.

This policy explains how AQYR Global Service handles information when you visit this website or contact our team.

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Who we are

“AQYR Global Service”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the team providing the services described on this website. For privacy enquiries, contact us at info@hiremiiglobal.com.

Information you choose to provide

If you make an enquiry, you may provide details such as your name, email address, phone number, company or occupation, the type of help you are seeking, and information you choose to include in your message. Please do not send passports, identity documents, health information or other sensitive records through a general website enquiry.

How the enquiry form works

The form currently prepares an email in your device’s email application. It does not send the enquiry by itself: you can review, change or discard the draft, and it is sent to us only if you choose to send it. If sent, the information is handled through the email systems used by you and our team.

We use enquiry information to respond, understand the assistance requested, assess whether our services may be suitable, communicate with you and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not use this website enquiry form to request sensitive identity or immigration documents.

Information collected when you visit

Our hosting, security and content-delivery services may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the website, such as an IP address, browser or device details, pages requested and request times. The site also stores your language choice in your browser’s local storage. This preference is used to remember English or Chinese on later visits; it is not a marketing cookie.

Some visual resources may be requested from third-party websites, including the AQYR brand asset and Google Fonts. Those providers may receive technical data from your browser when the resource is loaded. Their own privacy terms apply to that processing.

Disclosure, storage and access

We may use service providers to host, secure and operate the website or to communicate with you. Information may be processed or stored in Australia or other locations where those providers operate. We take reasonable steps appropriate to the circumstances, but the exact retention period depends on the nature of the enquiry, our operational needs and applicable legal requirements.

You may contact us to request access to or correction of personal information you have provided, or to raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity before responding. Contact info@hiremiiglobal.com.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the website or our practices change. The latest version will be published on this page. For questions about this policy, email info@hiremiiglobal.com.
