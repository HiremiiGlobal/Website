BEFORE YOU RECRUIT

Start with the role and the market.

A clear brief helps you decide where to search, what to offer and what needs closer review.

Define the work.

Discuss the responsibilities, team structure and reason for hiring. Separate essential skills from experience that can be developed on the job, so the brief reflects the work rather than a long wish list.

Consider the available talent.

Review relevant experience, transferable skills and available market information. Consider how salary, location, working arrangements and timing may affect the people willing and able to take the role.

Agree a workable next step.

Discuss which requirements are fixed and where there is flexibility before proceeding with a search or a candidate review. If sponsorship is relevant, assess the business, role and applicant together as a separate part of the work.

For an initial conversation, bring a position description, an indicative salary range, the work location and your preferred start date.
