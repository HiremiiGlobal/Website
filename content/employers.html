WHAT WE COORDINATE

Bring the moving parts together.

Know what needs to be reviewed, which information to prepare and who will coordinate each part of the work. The scope is agreed before we begin.

01

Role and business review

Review the duties, location, recruitment background and employment terms, then identify the employer information needed for the assessment.

02

Candidate and pathway assessment

Consider the candidate’s occupation, experience and visa circumstances alongside the business and role. Explain the pathway considerations and questions that need closer review.

03

Information and application preparation

Provide a case-specific preparation list and coordinate the employer and applicant records, following up on missing or inconsistent information.

04

Communication and follow-up

Coordinate information requests, explain what each party needs to provide and communicate relevant progress throughout the agreed work.

Can we contact you before we have a candidate?

Yes. We can start with the role and business needs, then discuss relevant talent connections and the information needed for a sponsorship assessment.

Can you assist with an employee we have already found?

Yes. Talent introductions are not a prerequisite for discussing sponsorship support. Share the role and employee’s background so we can review the matter.

What should we prepare for a first conversation?

A brief business overview, position description, work location and any candidate background available. Sensitive documents can be requested later through an agreed channel.
