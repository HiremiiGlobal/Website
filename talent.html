BEFORE YOU CONTACT US

A short overview is enough.

For an initial enquiry, tell us your visa or career goal, relevant experience and current situation. Please do not include identity documents or other sensitive records.

Can I enquire without an employer?

Yes. We can discuss your background and whether there may be suitable employer connections. Introductions depend on available roles and the interests of both parties.

Can you help with a visa unrelated to employment?

Yes. We welcome visitor, student, partner, family and other Australian visa enquiries as well as skilled and sponsored migration matters.

Can you review a complicated situation?

Yes. Start with a short explanation of the issue. We can identify the information needed to assess the matter before discussing a suitable service scope.

SKILLS, EDUCATION & CAREER

Connect what you have with what comes next.

Your qualifications and experience matter alongside your visa plans. We help identify what to prepare and where further advice is needed.

Skills assessment & qualification recognition.

We help organise qualification and employment information for the relevant assessment pathway, clarify preparation priorities and coordinate supporting records. Decisions are made by the responsible assessing authority.

Education-to-employment planning.

We discuss how study, training and skills development may fit your occupation and longer-term goals, alongside relevant visa considerations. Any course or provider arrangements are explored around your circumstances; study does not guarantee a job or migration outcome.
